Privacy Policy

Voice Vault · Last updated: October 13, 2026

Overview

Voice Vault is a voice recorder app. We do not require an account and we do not collect your personal information on any server.

Your Recordings

  • Recordings, note names and dates are stored only on your device.
  • We do not upload, share or sell your recordings.
  • Deleting a note removes it from your device. Uninstalling the app removes all app data.

Permissions

  • Microphone: needed to record voice notes and to transcribe them. The app only listens when you start recording or transcription.

Speech-to-Text

Transcription uses the speech recognition service built into your Android device (for example, Google's). Depending on your device settings, this service may process audio on your device or send it to its provider. This is handled by that service under its own privacy policy, not by us. Transcription is optional.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • No contacts, messages, call logs or location.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Security

Your data stays on your device and is protected by your device's security. Please keep your device locked and up to date.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page.
